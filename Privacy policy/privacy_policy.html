Privacy Policy

Screenshot to Action

Developed by Innovexa Technologies

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Screenshot to Action, developed by Innovexa Technologies. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information.

2. Information We Access

Our application may access or process:

  • Images and screenshots selected by users.
  • Text extracted from selected screenshots.
  • Detected phone numbers, website links, addresses, and dates.
  • History information saved by users.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide screenshot text recognition, detect useful information, and allow users to interact with detected phone numbers, websites, addresses, and dates.

4. Data Processing and Storage

Text recognition is performed on the user's device. Selected screenshots and extracted text are processed locally. Saved History records and screenshot copies are stored in the application's local storage.

The application does not require users to create an account.

5. Third-Party Applications

Users may choose to open detected information in external applications such as Phone, Browser, Maps, or Calendar. These applications have their own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

The application does not provide a feature to sell or share saved History or extracted screenshot content with third parties.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved History remains on the device until users delete individual records, clear all History, or uninstall the application.

8. Permissions

The application may request permissions necessary for its features, including selecting images from the device.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored within the application. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Screenshot to Action is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change.
